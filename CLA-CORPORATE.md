# Recurse Corporate Contributor License Agreement v1.0

Thank you for contributing to Recurse.

This agreement ("Agreement") covers Contributions made to the Project by
a company and the individuals it authorizes. It sets out the rights your
company ("Company") grants, and the commitments Recurse makes in return.
It follows the structure of the Apache Software Foundation Corporate
Contributor License Agreement, with one deliberate addition: a commitment
that the project's core stays open source (Section 4).

This Agreement is a companion to the Recurse Individual Contributor
License Agreement ("Individual CLA", see CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md). Company and
its authorized contributors **keep ownership of their contributions**.
Nothing here assigns copyright to Recurse.

Sign once per Company through the Project's CLA tooling, or by email to
ayush25khanna@gmail.com with the details in Section 12.

## 1. Definitions

**"Company"** (also **"You"**) means the legal entity that accepts this
Agreement, together with each Authorized Contributor identified under
Section 8.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
modification or addition to an existing work, that is intentionally
submitted to the Project by an Authorized Contributor on behalf of
Company for inclusion in, or for use by, the Project. "Submit" means any
form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the Project
or its representatives, including pull requests, commits, patches, issue
attachments, and messages on project channels. It does not include
anything conspicuously marked, in writing, as "Not a Contribution".

**"Authorized Contributor"** means an individual identified under
Section 8 who is authorized to submit Contributions on behalf of Company.

**"Project"** means the Recurse project, including the software,
documentation, and other materials that Recurse distributes as part of
it.

**"Recurse"** means the steward of the Project, currently Aayush Khanna,
and any successor or assignee that succeeds to the Project under
Section 9.

**"Project License"** means the license under which the Project is made
available to the public, currently the Apache License, Version 2.0.
References to the Project License mean that license as it may be changed
in accordance with Section 4.

**"Source"** and **"Object"** form have the meanings given to them in
the Project License.

not license or distribute any Contribution under terms other than:

(a) the Project License; or

Initiative, and (ii) grants every recipient at least the rights to use,
reproduce, modify, and redistribute the Contribution that the Project
License grants.

This commitment binds Recurse and its successors and assigns.

This commitment applies to the Contribution itself, and not to other
works with which the Contribution is distributed. Recurse may license or
distribute a Contribution together with, or as part of, a larger work
that includes software under other terms, provided the Contribution
itself remains available under a license described in (a) or (b).

Contribution as a hosted or managed service, using Contributions

## 9. Assignment and successors

Recurse may assign this Agreement, and the licenses granted under it, in
whole or in part, to any entity that succeeds to substantially all of the
Project, including through merger, acquisition, reorganization, or
conversion into a corporate entity, provided the successor agrees in
writing to be bound by Section 4.

Section 4 binds Recurse and its successors and assigns.

A change in the identity of Recurse, including the incorporation of a
company (for example, a future Recurse Labs) that takes over stewardship
of the Project, does not require Company to accept a new agreement.

For clarity, this Agreement does not itself transfer ownership of the
Project, or of any copyright in the Project, from the Project's founder
or its contributors to any company. Any such transfer requires a separate
written instrument, such as a founder intellectual property assignment
executed when a company is incorporated. This Agreement does not depend
on whether such a transfer occurs, and is not affected by it.
